# Spec: "What Do I Write?" - the blank-page guide

Status: **draft.** A newcomer who's set up their circle and reached a quiet home, or opened the composer for the first time, hits the hardest moment in any writing product: the blank page. This gives them permission and examples so they actually write, and it's what fills the new-user home's void with inspiration instead of an apology for being empty.

## Goal

Lower the blank-page barrier to near zero. The job is not to teach features - it's to make someone feel "oh, I can just write about that" and start. It shows up at two moments: the compose screen, and the new-user home when there's nothing more motivating to show. Both link to a short, revisitable guide page with an honest note in the creator's voice and real examples.

The feeling to protect: relief and permission. Never homework, never a pressure to perform.

## In scope

- **A "What do I write?" affordance** at the compose moment (a quiet link in the editor) and as a compact card on the new-user home - shown only when the person hasn't posted and there's no circle content to show instead.
- **A dedicated guide page** (revisitable, linkable from both entry points) holding: an honest, human note in the creator's voice (this is your canvas, the stakes are low, short is great, have fun with it); concrete examples of what people actually write - a book review, a small life update, the satirical festival dispatch, a shared-journal glimpse; and a few real example posts where available. A "Write" action at the end, so the guide converts rather than dead-ends.
- **Reusable everywhere.** The affordance isn't first-run-only; anyone stuck at the composer can reach it.

## Explicitly out (deliberately, for v1)

- **Auto-filling prompts or templates that write for you.** This is inspiration, not a form.
- **Gamified daily prompts or streaks.** Against the ethos.
- **A feature tour.** This is about *what to say*, not how the app works.

## States & behaviour

- **New-user home, nothing queued.** A compact "What do I write?" card with a couple of example teasers, sitting beside the Write action. It appears only when it's genuinely the best thing to show (not-yet-posted, and no circle content queued for Sunday) - it never competes with real Coming Sunday content.
- **Compose screen, any user.** A quiet "What do I write?" link that opens the guide, available to anyone, not just newcomers.
- **The guide page.** Calm and readable: the note, then examples, then a Write action. Revisitable and linkable, so it can even be shared as "here's what Rogha is for."

## Rules & edges

- **The guide is a real page**, not a one-time modal - someone can come back to it.
- **The home card yields to real content.** It only fills a genuine void; a populated Coming Sunday always wins the space.
- **Calm and permissive in tone.** It lowers stakes; it never pressures.

## Open questions

- **Example-post sourcing.** Real, consented posts vs. written examples - shares the onboarding value-moment's sourcing dependency. Written examples can carry a first version.
- **Full page vs. sheet.** Lean full page: revisitable, linkable from several places, even shareable.
- **The creator's note - signed and personal?** Lean yes; the original braindump wanted an honest message from you specifically, which is a big part of why it lands.

## Later, not now

- Rotating example sets, so the guide stays fresh on repeat visits.
- A richer prompts library, only if newcomers still stall after the note and examples.
