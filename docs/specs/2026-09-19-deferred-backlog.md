# Backlog: Deferred items across the circle-first initiative

Status: **living backlog, not a spec.** The small, deliberately-deferred pieces from across the circle-first chunks, gathered so none of them is lost. Each is a one-liner with its source; promote any to its own spec when its time comes. Nothing here blocks the shipping chunks.

## Invite-by-link
- **Request-to-join** - an ask-first path, upgrading both the expired-link dead-end and the no-approval model.
- **Removed-then-rejoin** - decide whether removal blocks the same invite for that person until re-invited (open question in the invite spec).
- **Seamless app-install resume** (deferred deep linking or an App Clip preview), only if the code fallback feels clunky in practice. No third-party tracking, per the ethos.
- **Desktop QR** - hand an invite from desktop to phone.
- **Flood cap / abuse throttle** beyond the basic code-entry rate-limiting.

## Multi-circle sharing (from the reconciled spec)
- **Empty-audience fallback** - decide whether deleting all of a post's target circles should revert it to draft rather than silently narrowing it to author-only.
- **Post-publish audience editing** - surface it deliberately as "share wider," or block it and route through Republish.
- **The "& others" wording and the orange colour exception** - confirm both, or hand to a copy pass.
- **Back-catalog migration** - fold old "posted the same thing three times" posts into single multi-circle posts.
- **Circle visual identity** (colour/font) and how it renders on a multi-circle post.
- **Siloed / per-circle comment threads**, only if the merged room ever proves too leaky.

## Onboarding / web
- **Web identity minimum** - confirm name required, avatar optional and addable later.
- **Email re-engagement for web-only users** - the Sunday reveal nudge via email, and the consent / verified-email around it.
- **Testimonial sourcing and curation** - how real example posts and member testimony are gathered, consented, and kept fresh (the onboarding value moment depends on this).
- **Full-screen welcome vs inline home** for the cold arrival (open in the onboarding spec).

## Circle notifications

- **Rename delivery** - push, or in-app only.
- **Per-circle mute** - if arrivals get noisy in a large circle.
- **"Recently joined" faces** on the circle surface as a calm reflection.
